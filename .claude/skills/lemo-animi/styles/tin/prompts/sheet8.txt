Every cell looks like the wind-up toy was moved to its next mechanical position and photographed: the body is rigid, never bending, squashing or stretching; only the riveted joints rotate. Repeated motions have the same amplitude every time, like a machine. The printed face never deforms; emotion comes from head tilt and body pose. The wind-up key stays plugged into the same spot on the back and turns a step further about its shaft in every cell.
