The design is assembled from a few stamped tin pieces: the body is a cylinder or egg shape, the head one piece, the limbs tin plates riveted to the body that can rotate only at the rivets. Eyes, mouth and clothing patterns are flat printed graphics in 2–3 print colours. A golden butterfly-shaped wind-up key sticks straight out of the centre of the back on a short shaft; draw it in all four views at that same spot: in the side view it points straight back, in the back view it faces the camera, in the front and three-quarter views it is mostly hidden behind the body (at most the wing tips peek out behind it), never sticking out of a side or the chest.
