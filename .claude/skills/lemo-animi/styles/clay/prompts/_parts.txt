[held_prop] Draw the prop in the character's hand from the same camera position as the set: the camera is slightly above the prop's rim, so the opening of a pot, bowl or plate is a very flat ellipse (the back rim and a little of the inside visible; not a top-down view), with the inside in a colour that contrasts strongly with the outside. Contents are drawn only inside the container; in the cells where the contents fly out, the container is empty, and nothing is drawn flying through the air.
[prop_view] Use the same viewpoint as the shot the prop will be composited into: the camera is at the character's chest-to-eye height, slightly above props on the table or in hand, looking down about 15 degrees; not a top-down view. A prop that is also needed seen from above is listed separately and marked "top-down"; draw that one from above.
