Effects are made of yarn too, fully opaque (no glass, nothing translucent). For bubbles: each bubble is a solid, slightly flattened ball crocheted in very pale sky-blue or white fine yarn, its surface in rounds of stitches spreading out from the centre, with a white yarn crescent highlight and a small dot highlight sewn on at the upper left; not a hollow ring. For splashes: clusters of white yarn tassels. Draw only what the Effect line above lists; add no extra stars, pompoms or loose yarn ends.
