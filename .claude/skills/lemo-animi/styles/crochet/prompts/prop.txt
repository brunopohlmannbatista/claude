Props that grow from the ground, such as seaweed or tassels, are drawn as a clump with a flat, level base growing upward (so it can sway left and right around its base). For a version bent by current or wind, the base stays firmly rooted in place and level; only the upper half bends over. Swimming creatures such as small fish or jellyfish are drawn in full side profile. Bubbles are drawn as solid, slightly flattened balls (pale sky-blue fine yarn, rounds of stitches, a white crescent highlight at the upper left).
