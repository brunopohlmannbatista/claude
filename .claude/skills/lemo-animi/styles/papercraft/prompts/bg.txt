The set is like a paper-cut lightbox: at least 4 paper layers staggered front to back (sky farthest, then distant, middle and near layers), each layer one whole cut strip of flat paper, with gaps and clear shadows between layers. Farther layers are paler and greyer, nearer layers deeper and more saturated. Camera front-on at eye level, paper layers parallel to the picture plane, never viewed at an angle. The frontmost paper layer is only a very narrow strip along the bottom edge of the frame (nearer foreground layers are composited later); it must not cover the footing.
