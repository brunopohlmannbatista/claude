Layered papercraft stop-motion assets. Material: paper sculpture cut, carved, folded and glued from coloured card and handmade paper, showing paper fibres, creases, knife cuts and small fuzzy edges. Every piece is a flat sheet of paper with real thickness, the pieces staggered front to back, like a real paper-cut lightbox photographed on a tabletop set, not 3D renders, not digital painting. Light comes from behind and from behind to the side, so paper edges glow with a rim of light and clear shadows fall between front and back pieces. Palette: layered cool tones of one family, deep blue, indigo and teal, with focal points in cream white and warm yellow (moon, sail, lamp).
