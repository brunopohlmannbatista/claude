Every cell looks like the paper puppet was reposed and photographed again. Paper is stiff: movement comes from whole paper pieces rotating, tilting and bending at the creases; no clay-like squash and stretch, no soft bending. Expressions come from swapping one facial-feature paper piece (eyes curved into arcs, mouth open); every cell shows a visible change. When the character sits in a vehicle such as a boat or cart, draw the whole vehicle in every cell, the same size and equally level, with anything upright on it (mast, sail, flag) always on the same side behind the character. Do not draw water or ground (the vehicle's tilt is rotated later in code).
