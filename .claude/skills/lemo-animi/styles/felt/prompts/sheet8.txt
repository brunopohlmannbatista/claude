Every cell looks like the felt puppet was reposed and photographed again. Felt puppets are soft with few joints: movement comes from the whole body leaning forward, leaning back, curling up and stretching out, in big amplitudes, but the silhouette always stays rounded. Slight squashing is fine; no clay-like stretching or deformation. Soft parts (a scarf or long ears, if any) swing up and fall with the movement, one beat behind the body.
