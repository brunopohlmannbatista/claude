Medium-close views must clearly show wool fibres and the small highlights on the bead eyes. A felt face cannot open a big mouth: expressions come from the eyes (bead eyes, or squinted into two curved yarn lines), blush only if the character sheet has it, ears raised or drooping, the body shrinking in or puffing up. When there are two character reference images, draw both characters in the same cell, with the left-right positions and height ratio as written.
