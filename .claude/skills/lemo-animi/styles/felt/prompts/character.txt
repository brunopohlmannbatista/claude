Design like a few felt balls put together: round head, round body, short arms and legs; eyes are small black beads; nose and mouth are embroidered in yarn or a small piece of pink felt. Scarves and hats are knitted yarn or felt in warm red or warm yellow that contrast with the cold night scene. A white or pale character needs one or two darker areas (inside of the ears, scarf, mittens) so it does not merge into the snow.
