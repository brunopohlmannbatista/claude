[held_prop] Draw the prop in the character's hand from the same camera position as the set: for things with an opening, such as baskets or bowls, the camera is slightly above the opening, so it is a very flat ellipse (the back rim visible); not a top-down view.
[prop_view] Use the same viewpoint as the shot the prop will be composited into: in medium and medium-close shots the camera is at eye level close to the ground, slightly above things on the ground, so the opening of anything with an opening is a very flat ellipse. A prop that is also needed seen from above is listed separately and marked "top-down"; draw that one from above.
