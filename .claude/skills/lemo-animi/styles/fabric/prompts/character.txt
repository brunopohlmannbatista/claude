Fabric puppet design: a sewn, rounded silhouette with a line of stitches visible along the seams; eyes are two buttons; nose and mouth embroidered in dark thread. The body is one plain or small-print fabric; accessories (neckerchief, straw hat, small cloth bag) are a different printed fabric or straw weave. At most two printed fabrics on the whole body, with small patterns; the silhouette is recognisable at a glance (e.g. a sheep is a puff of off-white curly fabric, a chick is a yellow fabric ball), so it does not blur into a blob when tiny in an extreme-wide shot.
