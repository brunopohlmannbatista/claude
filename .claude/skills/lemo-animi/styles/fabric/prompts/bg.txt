The set is a sewn patchwork countryside on a tabletop: fields are patches of different printed fabrics sewn together, the seams lines of stitches; paths are a line of stitching or a fabric ribbon; trees and houses are cotton-stuffed fabric toys; the sky is a piece of pale blue linen. Extreme-wide shots (showing the whole countryside and the route) look down at an angle from above, like viewing a patchwork map from a hillside; wide, medium and medium-close shots have the camera at eye level close to the ground, with the weave and stitches on the ground clearly visible.
