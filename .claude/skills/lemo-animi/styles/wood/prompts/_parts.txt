[held_prop] Draw the prop in the character's hand from the same camera position as the set: the camera is slightly above the prop's rim, so the opening of a pot, bowl or plate is a very flat ellipse (the back rim and a little of the inside visible; not a top-down view), with the inside in a colour that contrasts strongly with the outside.
[prop_view] Use the same viewpoint as the shot the prop will be composited into: the camera is mostly at the character's chest-to-eye height, slightly above props on the table or in hand, so the opening of a pot, bowl or plate is a very flat ellipse (the back rim and a little of the inside visible), not a top-down view. For a container that will hold something, make the inside a colour that contrasts strongly with the outside. A prop that is also needed seen from above is listed separately and marked "top-down"; draw that one from above.
